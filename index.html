What Is Rainwater Harvesting?

Rainwater harvesting is the process of collecting and storing rainwater from rooftops and other surfaces for future use. It helps conserve water and replenish groundwater. Collected water can be used for gardening, cleaning, and irrigation. Drinking requires appropriate treatment.

Methods of Rainwater Harvesting

🏠

Rooftop Harvesting

Collect rainwater from rooftops using pipes and tanks.

🌱

Groundwater Recharge

Direct rainwater into recharge pits to replenish groundwater.

🏞️

Surface Runoff

Collect rainwater in ponds and suitable storage structures.
